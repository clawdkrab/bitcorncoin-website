Chapter one · The Harvest

Corn is money.
Always has been.

Long before memes, people paid in grain. Then crypto Twitter started calling bitcoin "corn," somebody drew a white cob on an orange coin, and a farm full of parody trading cards grew out of the jokes. The old "BitCornect" bit, "WASA WASA, great harvest time," still echoes across the field. $BITCORN is that crop, replanted on Solana.

Chapter four · Planting Season

Four seeds to a full stalk

Scroll and watch them sprout. Each step is a seed. By the fourth one you have a field.

Seed 01

Get Phantom

Download the Phantom wallet (or any Solana wallet). Write the seed phrase on paper and never share it with anyone.

Seed 02

Add some SOL

Buy SOL on an exchange or in the wallet, then send it to your Phantom address. Keep a little extra for fees.

Seed 03

pump.fun or Jupiter

Open pump.fun while it's on the bonding curve, or Jupiter once it has graduated. Connect your wallet.

Seed 04
